About the Project

What is the FRA Atlas?

The FRA Atlas Digital Model consolidates individual and community forest rights claims filed under the Forest Rights Act, 2006, into a single geo-referenced record. Field data, scanned title deeds, and revenue maps are digitised, verified, and layered onto satellite imagery — giving officials, gram sabhas, and claimants a shared, transparent view of the process from application to patta.
